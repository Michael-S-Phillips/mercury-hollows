The derived products redistribute or are computed from these data sets; cite them together with this record:

- Bickel, V. T., Deutsch, A. N., and Blewett, D. T. (2025). Hollows on Mercury: Creation and analysis of a global reference catalog with deep learning. *Journal of Geophysical Research: Machine Learning and Computation*, 2, e2024JH000431. doi:10.1029/2024JH000431 (hollow detections; positions recomputed here).
- Herrick, R. R., Bateman, E. M., Crumpacker, W. G., and Bates, D. (2018). Observations from a global database of impact craters on Mercury with diameters greater than 5 km. *Journal of Geophysical Research: Planets*, 123, 2089–2109. doi:10.1029/2017JE005516 (crater catalog).
- Orgel, C., Fassett, C. I., Michael, G., Riedel, C., van der Bogert, C. H., and Hiesinger, H. (2020). Re-examination of the population, stratigraphy, and sequence of Mercurian basins. *Journal of Geophysical Research: Planets*, 125, e2019JE006212. doi:10.1029/2019JE006212 (basins).
- Blewett, D. T., and others (2016). Analysis of MESSENGER high-resolution images of Mercury's hollows and implications for hollow formation. *Journal of Geophysical Research: Planets*, 121, 1798–1813. doi:10.1002/2016JE005070 (hollow depths).
- Nittler, L. R., and others (2020). Global major-element maps of Mercury from four years of MESSENGER X-Ray Spectrometer observations. *Icarus*, 345, 113716. doi:10.1016/j.icarus.2020.113716 (X-ray ratio maps, from the NASA Planetary Data System).
- Becker, K. J., and others (2016). First global digital elevation model of Mercury. 47th Lunar and Planetary Science Conference, abstract 2959 (topography used for terrain and slope classes).
- Denevi, B. W., and others (2018). Calibration, projection, and final image products of MESSENGER's Mercury Dual Imaging System. *Space Science Reviews*, 214, 2. doi:10.1007/s11214-017-0440-y (MDIS mosaics and images).
- Pegg, D. L., Rothery, D. A., Balme, M. R., and Conway, S. J. (2021). Explosive vent sites on Mercury: Commonplace multiple eruptions and their implications. *Icarus*, 365, 114510. doi:10.1016/j.icarus.2021.114510 (volcanic vent catalog).

MESSENGER images, navigation data, and X-Ray Spectrometer maps are from the NASA Planetary Data System.
