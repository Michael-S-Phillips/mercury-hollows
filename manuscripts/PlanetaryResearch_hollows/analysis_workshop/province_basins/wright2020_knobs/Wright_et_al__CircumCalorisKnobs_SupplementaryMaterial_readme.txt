##############################

ATTRIBUTE TABLE FIELD HEADINGS

##############################

FID - standard ArcGIS automatically assigned shape specific object identifier

SHAPE* - standard geometry field (point)

KnobID - Unique knob identifier field

Latitude - Latitude of point identifying knob

Longitude - Longitude of point identifying knob

KnobType - Qualitative knob classification. Certain = conical knob. Uncertain = domal knob (probably a degraded ejecta block, but could be some other form of hummock). <5 km = A possible knob, but originally measured (ArcGIS measure tool) to be too small to be investigated morphometrically.

NAC - MDIS product ID of Narrow Angle Camera image used to inspect knob morphology (where available).

WAC - MDIS product ID of Wide Angle Camera image used for shadow measurements (where available).

ShadSlopeN - Max slope measured on the northern flank of a given knob from its shadow (see paper for explanation of measurement technique).

ShadSlopeS - Max slope measured on the southern flank of a given knob from its shadow (see paper for explanation of measurement technique).

MLA - MDIS product ID of Mercury Laser Altimeter data used to measure knob topography (where available).

MLAslopeN - Max slope measured on the northern flank of a given knob from its available MLA data (see paper for explanation of measurement technique).

MLAslopeS - Max slope measured on the southern flank of a given knob from its available MLA data (see paper for explanation of measurement technique).

MjrAxis_km - Major axis length of a knob measured in kilometers. This measurment was made after the original qualitative knob classification where knob sizes were approximately measured using the ArcGIS measurement tool.

NAC_res_m - Spatial resolution of NAC image (where available) measured in meters used to inspect knob morphology.
